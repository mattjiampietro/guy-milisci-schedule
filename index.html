🔍 UT Course Schedule Searcher Playbook Rules, Course Numbers & Core Component 010

1️⃣ Filter 3: Communication 010 (C1)

To find writing-intensive core courses on the UT Registrar Schedule: Select Core Curriculum, then scroll to the 3rd filter dropdown and select Communication (010). Any C1 course fulfills the Texas Core communication requirement.

2️⃣ The UT Middle Number Rule (Upper Div)

UT Austin course numbers have 3 digits (e.g. CMS 322E). If the middle digit is between 2 and 8 (e.g., 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8), it is Upper Division. Digits 0 or 1 indicate lower division (freshman/sophomore), and 9 indicates graduate level.

3️⃣ Plan II Biology: Dr. Ruth Buskirk

Guy must take a Plan II natural science. BIO 301E with Dr. Ruth Buskirk is the gold standard: legendary teaching, explicit study guide objectives, and generous review sessions. Meets MWF 1-2 PM with Tuesday discussion.
